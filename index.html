Anti-Self-Distillation for Reasoning RL
via Pointwise Mutual Information

Reverse the sign. Reach a strong GRPO baseline in 2–10× fewer steps, with up to +11.5 points of final accuracy.

The diagnosis. Default self-distillation's per-token reward is conditional pointwise mutual information — it rewards shortcut tokens the verified solution already implies and penalizes the deliberation tokens (Wait, Let, Maybe) that drive multi-step search.
The fix. AntiSD ascends a bounded Jensen–Shannon divergence between student and teacher instead of descending reverse KL — flipping the per-token sign at the source — with a single auto-calibrated entropy-triggered gate. A drop-in replacement, no extra cost.
The result. Across five 4B–30B models (dense + MoE) on math & code reasoning, AntiSD beats GRPO on every model and stacks on top of saturated GRPO checkpoints.

An oracle-conditioned teacher biases the student toward a single solution root; reversing the signal preserves deliberation and recovers the rest of the search tree.

One line of code

The entire change, at its core, is a sign flip on the self-distillation objective — from descending the divergence to ascending it.

loss =  KL(student || teacher)   # default self-distillation (descend)
loss = -KL(student || teacher)   # AntiSD (ascend) — literally "train against it"

In the paper we ascend a Jensen–Shannon divergence rather than plain reverse KL: the JSD shape is asymmetrically bounded, which absorbs the heavy-tailed deliberation spikes that pure KL ascent cannot.

The Diagnosis: a structural shortcut bias

Conditioning the teacher on a verified solution turns it into an oracle. We trace the per-token signal $u_t = t_t - s_t$ and find it splits tokens into two regimes.

$$ u_t \;=\; \log \frac{\pi_\theta(y_t \mid x, c, y_{

The per-token reward is the conditional pointwise mutual information between the next token and the privileged context $c$. Default self-distillation uses $\delta_t = +u_t$ — rewarding whatever $c$ makes more likely.

Per-token signal $u_t = t_t - s_t$ on Qwen3-4B-IT-2507 at AIME–25. Shortcut tokens ($u_t \gg 0$, red) — Given, Assign, succeeds, holds — are strongly rewarded once the answer is known. Deliberation tokens ($u_t \ll 0$, blue) — Wait, Let, Maybe, Alternatively — are strongly penalized, since $c$ has already committed to a solution. The heatmap (right) shows two off-diagonal lobes of opposite sign; the deliberation lobe is heavier and reaches $u_t \le -20$.

  Why standard self-distillation fails on math

Pulling the student toward this oracle teacher reinforces tokens that track the known solution and weakens the very tokens that drive search. This recasts the well-known "responses get shorter under self-distillation" observation not as benign compression, but as a structural shortcut — suppression concentrated exactly on the deliberation steps. The polarity is not specific to reverse KL: any convex $f$-divergence descent inherits the same shortcut/deliberation split.

The Method: ascend Jensen–Shannon divergence

AntiSD is three components stacked on the GRPO advantage. Sign reversal carries most of the gain; the JSD shape and entropy gate make it stable across model families.

$$ A_t^{\mathrm{AntiSD}} \;=\; -\,\varphi(u_t), \qquad \varphi(u) \;:=\; \tfrac{1}{2}\big(\mathrm{softplus}(u) - \log 2\big) $$
1

Reverse the direction

From observation O1 (wrong polarity), flip descent → ascent. Deliberation tokens ($u_t<0$) now receive positive advantage; shortcut tokens ($u_t>0$) receive negative advantage — the sign is flipped at the source.

2

Bound it with JSD

From observation O2 (asymmetric, heavy-tailed deliberation lobe), ascend JSD rather than reverse KL. Its $f$-divergence shape caps the advantage at $\tfrac{1}{2}\log 2$ on the over-sampled deliberation side while keeping a linear penalty on the shortcut side.

3

Gate on teacher entropy

Ascent is not self-terminating. A Schmitt-trigger gate disables the term once the teacher's per-token entropy collapses below $\tau_{\mathrm{down}} = 0.93\,H_{\mathrm{warm}}$ (auto-calibrated from 5 warmup steps), re-enabling on recovery. One shared multiplier, no per-model tuning.

  A training-free process reward model

The per-token signal is structurally a PRM — the difference $V_t - V_{t-1}$ of the model's own log-posterior $V_t = \log P(c \mid x, y_{\le t})$ for the privileged context $c$. The contributions telescope over a trajectory to the sequence-level PMI, placing AntiSD squarely in the framework of potential-based reward shaping: the shaping term leaves the set of optimal policies invariant, with no auxiliary annotation, learned value heads, or Monte-Carlo rollouts.

  It transfers to code

On the Dolci-RLZero code RL dataset (Qwen3-8B, avg@10), AntiSD improves over GRPO by +1.2 on HumanEval+ (41.6 vs 40.4) and +2.3 on MBPP+ (63.3 vs 61.0) — smaller than on math, but consistent in direction, in a setting where the trajectory reward is already denser.

Training Dynamics

AntiSD ignites earliest and stays in a stable entropy band; default SD diverges in opposite directions across model families.

Three models (rows) along six axes (columns). Faded traces are raw values; bold traces are 20-step rolling means. AntiSD's truncation-corrected train reward climbs from ~0.5 to ~0.95 within ~30 steps — a regime GRPO reaches only after ~150 steps and SD never reaches. SD's entropy collapses toward ~0.1 nats/token on Qwen3-4B-IT-2507 (over-confident on the shortcut template) and inflates past 1 nat/token on Olmo3-7B-IT — exactly the bidirectional failure mode the sign reversal addresses.

Ablations & Failure Modes

Each AntiSD component earns its place. Removing the teacher, the gate, or the sign reversal each produces a characteristic failure on Qwen3-4B-IT-2507.

Mechanism ablations. Top: reward over non-truncated rollouts. Bottom: mean response length. Line truncation = run termination after collapse. No-teacher (orange) collapses within ~70 steps via self-reinforcement — the strongest evidence the gain depends on the privileged-information identity, not generic log-prob shaping. No-gate (brown) ignites faster but collapses near step 90 on Qwen models as teacher entropy crosses zero, while surviving the full run on Olmo — the gate is cross-model insurance, not a per-model necessity.

  AntiSD stacks on saturated GRPO checkpoints

Resuming the Qwen3-8B GRPO run at its step-200 saturation point and continuing with AntiSD for just 30 post-resume steps essentially matches the from-base peak (65.0 vs 65.7 Avg) — 6× fewer steps than from-base AntiSD needs. The deliberation-token signal stays informative at the exact point GRPO's trajectory reward cannot push past: AntiSD complements GRPO rather than replacing it.
